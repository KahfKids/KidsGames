# Hextris: sound credits

Every sound is a real recording, cut, faded and levelled by `tools/sound-cut.py` from `audio/sfx/recipe.json`.
No music, no synthesis, no tuned or ringing sounds (no ceramic, glass or bells): only taps, knocks, wood, stone, cloth, paper, water and air.
The old page's synthesized sounds (`js/sounds.js`) were removed on 2026-11-16.

Source of all originals: [BigSoundBank](https://bigsoundbank.com), recordings by Joseph Sardin, CC0 1.0 (licence page
checked 2026-11-16; commercial use allowed, no attribution required). "Additional sounds: Joseph SARDIN - BigSoundBank.com".
The originals are kept outside the repo in `../KidsGames-sounds/bigsoundbank/`. Nothing new was downloaded for this game.
