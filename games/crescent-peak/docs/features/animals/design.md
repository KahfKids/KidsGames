# Animals, birds and insects

**Status:** designed · **Updated:** 2026-11-17 · [GDD](../../GDD.md)

In the reference, almost everyone you meet is an animal: birds, goats, rabbits, frogs, deer, raccoons, turtles and more. That is a big part of why the island feels alive and varied. Ours keeps that richness, about **50 kinds** of creature across 18 areas and every hour of the day. Something moves in every view, and the nature notebook rewards finding them all ([side activities](../side-activities/design.md)). Our people are human villagers ([characters](../characters/design.md)), so animals don't talk. They are helped by what the child does: water for a thirsty dog, turtle hatchlings guided to the sea, a stork chick lifted back to its nest ([quests](../quests/design.md)). That is the heart of the story: kindness to people and to every living thing ([story](../story/design.md)).

**No animal has a face** (rule 14): no eyes, no mouths. Each reads by its shape, colour, how it moves and its real recorded sound. No animal is ever hurt or scared on purpose.

## Rules
1. No eyes, mouths or faces on any animal. Heads are simple shapes; personality comes from movement.
2. Animals react to the child (move aside, flutter, follow) but never chase, bite or block. No wolves, bears, snakes or other scary predators.
3. No animal is hurt. Fish are caught and released, or given to someone who asked ([fishing](../fishing/design.md)). Creatures for the notebook are sketched, never caught.
4. Every animal sound is a real recording ([audio](../audio/design.md)). Many are already in our library: ducks, goats, frog, hen, rooster, cat.
5. Some creatures appear only at certain hours ([day and night](../day-night/design.md)): deer and the rooster at dawn, the fox, bats and swifts at dusk, owls, hedgehogs, moths and fireflies at night, turtle hatchlings on one night.
6. Dogs are a working sheepdog and a stray on the path. They stay outdoors, aren't pets in the house, and don't lick the child.
7. Helped animals stay near afterward: the harbour cat follows around the harbour, the dog rests by its full trough, the storks fly beside the glide home ([quests](../quests/design.md)).

## Where the models come from
**"Pack"** and **"Poly Pizza"** mean a free model is the starting point. Each is still re-shaped to our style, recoloured to our palette, cut to the triangle budget, and has its eyes removed in Blender, so models from different authors end up looking like one family. **"Make"** means we model it ourselves, because nothing free was found or because it is so simple (insects are 2–8 triangles) that making it is faster than fixing one.

| Free source (checked 2026-11-17) | Licence | What it covers for us |
|---|---|---|
| [Quaternius Ultimate Animated Animal Pack](https://quaternius.com/packs/ultimateanimatedanimals.html) | CC0 | 12 animals with 12+ animations each, in glTF: the big land animals (deer, fox, horse, dogs and others; check the list on download) |
| [Quaternius Farm Animal Pack](https://quaternius.com/packs/farmanimal.html) | CC0 | 7 animated farm animals: sheep, cow, horse and others |
| [Quaternius Animated Fish Pack](https://quaternius.itch.io/lowpoly-animated-fish) | CC0 | fish with a swim animation, including a **dolphin**, manta ray, shark and whale (only the dolphin and fish are used) |
| [Quaternius: all CC0 uploads](https://opengameart.org/content/all-cc0-uploader-quaternius) | CC0 | more animals (an eagle, a cat, dogs), the Cute Fish pack; check each |
| [Poly Pizza](https://poly.pizza/) | CC0 or CC-BY, per model | over 7,000 low-poly models. Found there: a **dolphin**, **turtles**, **bees**, **storks**, **gulls**, **crabs**, an **octopus**, a **hedgehog**, a **squirrel**, **camels**, **goats**. Many are "Poly by Google" (CC-BY 3.0: credit needed). Not animated, so we rig them in Blender |
| [Animated butterfly](https://opengameart.org/content/butterfly-animated) | CC0 | one rigged butterfly |
| still not found free | — | tortoise (a turtle can be reshaped), kingfisher, hoopoe, swifts, herons, pelicans, seals, and most insects: **we make these** |

**What the free sources cover now:** most of the list. All the big land animals, the dolphin and fish come animated (Quaternius, CC0). The sea life and many birds come unanimated from Poly Pizza, and we add simple rigs (a bird needs about 4 bones). What's left is mostly small birds and insects, which are quick to make. The cost of variety is build time, not frame time.

## How they're drawn cheaply (pillar 5)
- **Flocks and swarms** (sheep, butterflies, bees, fireflies, fish, birds far away) are drawn in batches, one draw per kind, moved in the vertex shader.
- **Insects** are tiny flat shapes, 2–8 triangles each, close to the child, and fade out beyond 25 m.
- **Animals** have at most 600 triangles. Most move in the vertex shader (hop, flap, graze, swim). Only the nearest 6 use bones, updated at 30 Hz.
- **Creatures load with the terrain chunks around the child** (a ring of chunks, loaded early and dropped late so crossing a border doesn't reload), one upload a frame, plus those of the current hour.
