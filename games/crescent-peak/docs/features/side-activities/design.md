# Side activities

**Status:** designed · **Updated:** 2026-11-17 · [GDD](../../GDD.md)

The reference keeps players for hours with things beside the climb: a ball game on the beach, parkour races, fishing, a boat, treasure maps and shell hunting. Ours has the same breadth, made our own, spread over the areas and hours of the day ([island layout](../island-layout/design.md), [day and night](../day-night/design.md)). Each activity is short, can be repeated, and rewards doing better. None can be failed in a way that takes something away, and none rewards a risky act a child could copy (D-021). [Fishing](../fishing/design.md) has its own note.

## Activities

| # | Activity | Where | When | How it plays | Reward |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Keep it up** | the beach court | day | knock a ball back and forth with the beach children without it touching the sand | 10 in a row: ribbon; 20: a sun hat; 30: lantern |
| 2 | **Flag runs** | 3 courses: along the village terrace walls to the harbour; the orchard terraces to the pond; the forest paths to the tower | day | race Yusra, the fastest runner on the island, through a course of flags on paths and low walls; she gets 5% faster each time you win; flaps are capped per course | first win on the first course: ribbon; the others: lanterns |
| 3 | **Follow the swifts** | glide courses through rings of swifts: the orchard, the gorge, and the summit down to the harbour | dusk | glide through the rings; each ring gives a gust of speed | the summit course: ribbon; the others: shawl patterns |
| 4 | **Shells for the sandcastle** | beaches and river banks and the reef | any | find 15 shells for the beach children's great sandcastle, which grows as you bring them | ribbon; at 5 shells, the goggles |
| 5 | **Nature notebook** | everywhere | creatures by hour | stand still near a creature for 2 s to sketch it, without catching it ([animals](../animals/design.md)) | lanterns at 12, 24 and 40 entries |
| 6 | **Lighting the lanterns** | the village | every night after the ending | light the street lanterns with a taper, ending at the lanterns of kindness | lantern patterns |
| 7 | **Kite flying** | the meadow, the outlook | windy afternoons | fly the kite you saved in the kite help; loops and dives in the wind | a journal star for a loop |
| 8 | **Stargazing** | the summit bench, the outlook telescope, the plateau camp | night | look up: the stars, and the moon's shape that night (the old crescent before dawn, the new one growing after the 29th) | notebook entries for the moon's shapes |
| 9 | **Iftar plates** | the village | each evening after the ending | Grandma fills a plate for a neighbour; carry it over before sunset. The neighbour waits, so nothing fails; the warmth is the reward (any hadith line is looked up first) | lanterns |
| 10 | **The goat race** | the old road | day | see [quests](../quests/design.md) | lantern |
| 11 | **Rowboat** | the harbour, the plateau lake | any | row with the stick; explore the islet, the reef, the far islet; a race round the harbour buoys with the fisher's son | the far islet; lantern for the race |
| 12 | **Rapids ride** | the river gorge | day | ride a wooden tub down the rapids to the pond, steering around rocks | a journal star for a clean run |
| 13 | **Snow tray sledding** | the snowy shoulder | any | slide down on a wooden tray through flags, over snow bumps | lantern |
| 14 | **Ice sliding** | the frozen tarn | any | run and slide; push curling stones to a target with the herders' children | a scarf |
| 15 | **Treasure maps** | 3 maps, hidden in the world | any | each map is a picture of a place; dig with the shovel where it shows ([tools](../tools/design.md)). Clues are pictures only | lanterns, a lantern pattern, a telescope lens |
| 16 | **Diving** | the reef, the pond | day | with goggles, dive under for shells and to see fish | shells, notebook entries |
| 17 | **Splash jumps** | the low diving rock at the pond | day | jump from the low rock for a big splash and the children's cheer; no rewards for height | — |

## Rules
1. Every activity is shown once by someone who does it (the beach children, Yusra, the fisher), with pictures, before it is tried.
2. Losing a race or dropping the ball never takes anything away; trying again is instant.
3. Each best score is saved and shown where the activity is (a chalk board on the beach, a flag at the race start).
4. Nothing to do with worship is an activity with a score (rule 17). Iftar and suhoor are moments, not tasks.
5. No activity rewards climbing onto roofs, into wells, or jumping from high places.
