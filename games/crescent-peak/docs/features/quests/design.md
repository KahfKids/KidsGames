# Quests (helps and hidden spots)

**Status:** designed · **Updated:** 2026-11-17 · [GDD](../../GDD.md)

Helps for people, helps for animals and hidden spots give most of the [ribbons](../ribbons/design.md) and many lanterns of kindness. The reference mixes fetch tasks, races and secrets; ours keeps that mix, and every task is an act of kindness, to people and to animals (pillar 3, and the story's heart: [story](../story/design.md)). Each task is short (1–5 minutes), is explained with pictures ([dialogue](../dialogue/design.md)), and can't fail. Nothing asks a child to do something risky they could copy (D-021).

## Helping animals
Animals can't ask, so these start when the child notices: a sound, an animal acting strangely, a villager saying "poor thing". When a help is done, a villager who saw it says thank you and gives the reward, or the child finds the ribbon nearby (never "from the wind", which would make nature the rewarder). Helped animals stay near afterward: the cat follows, the dog rests by its full bowl, the storks fly beside the glide home ([animals](../animals/design.md)).

## Rules
1. A task can be dropped and picked up again at any time; its progress is saved.
2. Carrying (the lamb, the bucket, the jar, the basket, the chick) disables glide and flap but allows walking, jumping and climbing.
3. Every reward is given by a person with a thank-you, or found tied somewhere. Never for worship (rule 17).
4. With 1 ribbon at the start, at least 4 more are possible right away (net floats, the gull, Keep it up, the lamb); with those, the rest open up. No route leaves a child without a reachable ribbon ([island layout](../island-layout/design.md)).
5. Collected things (apricots, cones) are counted on screen only while a task is active, as pictures.

## Edge cases
- Carrying the lamb into water: the child wades but can't swim; at the depth limit the lamb jumps out and waits on the shore.
- Dropping the oil jar in water: it floats back to the press (no loss).
- Taking the hard route to the summit without the oil: the imam has a spare wick; the finale works anyway.
