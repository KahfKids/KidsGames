# Shape Patterns: sound credits

Effects are real recordings, cut, faded and levelled by `tools/sound-cut.py` from `audio/sfx/recipe.json`.
No music or instrumental sounds. The old generated effects (`js/sounds.js`) were replaced on 2026-11-16.
Spoken prompts retain the device's speech synthesis. Arabic praise retains the existing shared speech clips
(`js/kit/praise-clips.js`, Microsoft ar-SA-HamedNeural via edge-tts); those clips are synthetic speech, not human recordings.

Source of all originals: [BigSoundBank](https://bigsoundbank.com), recordings by Joseph Sardin, CC0 1.0 (licence page
checked 2026-11-16; commercial use allowed, no attribution required). "Additional sounds: Joseph SARDIN - BigSoundBank.com".
The originals are kept outside the repo in `../KidsGames-sounds/bigsoundbank/`.
